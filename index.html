Privacy Policy

Thank you for playing Port Crane ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the app.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information such as your name, email address, physical location, or contacts.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Port Crane uses third-party advertising services (specifically Unity Ads) to display advertisements. These third-party services may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA, IP address, and device performance metrics) in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency and privacy guidelines to serve contextually relevant ads.

For more information on Unity's privacy practices, please visit Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not collect personal data directly, we do not store any user databases. Local game progress and high scores are stored locally on your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

Port Crane does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
